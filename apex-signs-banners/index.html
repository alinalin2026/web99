Apex Signs service

Banners

Banners are a fast way to put a large message exactly where people will see it — useful for launches, promotions, events, building sites and temporary campaigns.

Call 087 246 0416

Big messages without permanent installation

A banner can cover a large area without the cost or commitment of permanent fabricated signage. That makes it useful when the message, location or campaign may change.

Where banners work well

  • Business openings and launches
  • Seasonal promotions and sales
  • Events and sponsorship
  • Construction and development sites
  • Temporary directional signage
  • Indoor display and backdrop use

Design for viewing distance

Large does not automatically mean readable. A banner needs one strong message, enough contrast and a type size that works from the expected viewing distance.

Finishing for the installation

Eyelets, hems, pole pockets and other finishing choices depend on how and where the banner will be mounted. Planning this before print gives a cleaner installation and helps the banner stay tensioned properly.

Coordinate with the rest of the campaign

Banners can share the same artwork system as window graphics, posters and event displays, helping a temporary campaign look like a deliberate extension of the brand.
