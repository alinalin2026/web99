Questions

Before you get in touch.

You do not need to know the technical answer before contacting us. Describing the problem is enough.

Do you only work with new offices?
No. Workspace Solutions can help with existing workplaces too — upgrades, equipment, connectivity and day-to-day technical support.
Can you help if I don't know exactly what equipment I need?
Yes. Start with what you need the workspace or team to do. The equipment should follow the requirement, not the other way around.
Do you provide on-site support in Wexford?
The business is positioned around local Wexford support, including hands-on work where the job requires it. Exact coverage and response expectations should be confirmed when booking.
Can you source the equipment as well as install it?
Yes. Equipment sourcing is part of the service proposition, helping businesses avoid unnecessary specification and compatibility research.
What happens after installation?
After-sales support is part of the offer, so the relationship does not have to end when a new system or workspace is installed.
