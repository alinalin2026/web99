# Web99 — Pre-Delivery Checklist

Sent to the customer right after they pay, in one go, per the "collect
everything in one pass" rule. Nothing on this list should be chased
piecemeal mid-build — get it all before the 5-day delivery clock's work
actually starts.

## Every customer gets asked for this, regardless of category

1. Business name — exact spelling/branding to use
2. Logo — do you have one? Send it. If not, one gets designed as part
   of delivery (same process used for the free preview).
3. Address / area served
4. Phone number
5. Opening hours (if relevant to the business)
6. WhatsApp number for the site's contact button
7. Domain — already own one, or a name to register fresh?
8. Preferred email address(es) to set up (e.g. info@, hello@)
9. Existing brand colors/style, if any
10. Real photos of the business, team, or premises — used first choice
    over AI-generated images. AI images are the fallback only when
    real photos genuinely don't exist.
11. Anything from the original Sarah conversation that still needs
    confirming or was left vague

## Category-specific additions

**Emergency trades** (plumber, electrician, locksmith, tow)
- Service area / radius
- Response promise (e.g. 24/7, or specific hours)
- Any licenses/certifications to display

**Appointment trades** (barber, dentist, physio, nails)
- Full service list with prices
- Typical appointment duration (needed if Cal.com booking was
  purchased as an add-on)
- If Cal.com was purchased: confirmation they've created their own
  account and connected their calendar (their account, not ours —
  same ownership rule as Stripe Connect)

**Walk-in & hospitality** (café, restaurant, shop)
- Menu or product list with prices
- Any dietary/allergen info
- Map/location confirmed correct

**Considered purchases** (solicitor, accountant, builder, dance school)
- Credentials/certifications
- Examples of past work or a portfolio, if they have one
- Testimonials, if any exist (never invented if they don't)
- Years in business

**Product sellers** (florist, bakery, hot tub chemicals)
- Full product list with prices and photos
- Categories/collections wanted
- Any stock/availability notes worth mentioning
