# Web99 — Pricing & Services Reference

This is the source of truth for what's included, what's extra, and what
things cost. Sarah quotes from this — nothing outside this list is ever
improvised, and every price here is quoted verbatim.

## The pricing philosophy (why these numbers are low)

The €99 site is the anchor. Nothing sold after it should ever feel
bigger than the site itself — a customer should think "that's a nice
price", never "they charged €99 for the whole site and now want €149
for one page". Everything below is automated or near-automated, so the
margin holds even at these numbers. Low prices + generous small stuff =
customers who come back for years. That's the business.

## The rule

The €99 build is the acquisition product, not the business. Real work
past initial delivery is a paid request through Sarah — but see the
free-forever rule below, which comes first. No retainers, no accounts —
customers pay only when they need something, and every "something" has
a fixed price here so it never has to be worked out live.

## Included in every €99 build (one-time, applies to any business type)

- The site itself — one front page + basic pages, built to what the
  customer describes
- Domain + hosting — first year included
- 3 months of Facebook posts (30 posts — 10 a month, one every 3
  days — written once, delivered as a batch). Posts only: setting up
  the Facebook Page itself is NOT included (€49 extra, below).

No ecommerce, no bookings, no admin panel — those are add-ons below.

## FREE, FOREVER — never charged, never counted against anything

Small factual updates to what's already on the site are always free,
for the life of the relationship. This is deliberate customer
experience, not an oversight. Free means:

- Changing opening hours
- Updating a phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address
- Fixing a spelling mistake or the business name
- Updating an address
- Changing a single existing price on a menu/service list
- Swapping one photo they send for one already on the site

The test: **correcting or updating a fact that's already there = free.
Anything that creates something new = priced.** New sections, new
pages, new text written from scratch, design/layout changes, batches
of changes, "can you redo the…" — that's the paid list below.

## The 3 free edits (after delivery)

On top of free-forever factual updates, every customer gets 3 free
edit rounds after delivery for the bigger-than-factual stuff — moving
things around, colour changes, rewording a section. Not rebuilds. When
the 3 are used, the 4th request is never refused — it's quoted from
the list below, plainly.

## Custom requests (online shops and anything unusual)

Never turned down. An online shop, payments, bookings, logins or any
custom feature is a YES as a custom project with its own custom quote.
Sarah never names a price or range; she says it needs a custom quote
and that someone from the team will get back to them by email (they
can also write to hello@web99.ie). The standard site is still built
and shown to them first.

## What Sarah never does

- Never charges for anything in the free-forever list — and never
  makes the customer feel like they got away with something. Free
  means cheerfully free.
- Never promises something not on this list without "someone from the
  team will email a quote before anything starts"
- Never gives a price or range for a shop or other custom request —
  it goes to the team for a custom quote
- Never accepts or stores a customer's password, API key, or any
  account credential
