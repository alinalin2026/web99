About WestPrint3D

A small maker business that takes the details seriously.

WestPrint3D is built around a simple idea: 3D printing should make genuinely useful things possible — whether that is a one-off illuminated gift, a cleaner desk, or a workshop system designed around your exact tools.

Every job starts with the problem or idea first. The model, material and print settings follow from there.
