About Workspace Solutions

Local technology support built around the workplace, not the ticket number.

Workspace Solutions brings office setup, business technology, equipment and ongoing support together under one local point of contact in Wexford.

The idea is simple

Small and growing businesses often do not need a complicated IT procurement process. They need somebody who can understand the workspace, recommend what is appropriate, get it working and remain available afterwards.

That is the position Workspace Solutions is designed to fill: practical support for the technology around a working business.

Local by design

Being based in Wexford matters when a project needs a site visit, equipment needs to be installed or a problem is easier to solve in person. Remote help has its place, but it should not be the only option.

More than the installation

The relationship should continue after the new equipment is switched on. After-sales support is part of the offer so businesses have somewhere familiar to turn when needs change or something stops working as expected.
