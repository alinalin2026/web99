What this service is for

Workspace Solutions can help source hardware and office technology around the actual use case, compatibility and budget rather than leaving the business to choose from hundreds of nearly identical products.

Equipment can include

  • Desktop and laptop computers
  • Monitors and multi-screen setups
  • Printers and shared office devices
  • Keyboards, mice, docks and peripherals
  • Networking equipment
  • Other practical office technology requirements

Buy for the job it needs to do

A good equipment recommendation considers who will use it, which software or devices it must work with, how long it should remain useful and what support will be needed afterwards.

How we approach it

Start with a short conversation or the setup check. We clarify the outcome, look at the current environment where relevant, recommend a right-sized approach and agree the work before installation or changes begin.
