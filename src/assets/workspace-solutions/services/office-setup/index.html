Complete Office Setup

Get a workspace ready for people to work — without coordinating five different suppliers.

Call 089 272 1465

What this service is for

A practical office setup combines the physical workspace with the technology people actually need: connectivity, workstations, screens, printers, peripherals and the infrastructure around them.

What can be included

  • Workspace technology planning around team size and roles
  • Computers, monitors, peripherals and practical workstation equipment
  • Network and Wi‑Fi requirements
  • Printers and shared office technology
  • Installation, setup and testing
  • Coordination with existing infrastructure and suppliers

A new office should feel ready on day one

Instead of buying equipment first and finding out later that pieces do not work well together, start with the people, the space and the tasks. Workspace Solutions can help translate those requirements into a sensible setup and install it as one coherent system.

How we approach it

Start with a short conversation or the setup check. We clarify the outcome, look at the current environment where relevant, recommend a right-sized approach and agree the work before installation or changes begin.
