Digital Printing

Large-format print turns artwork into practical graphics for signs, promotions, windows, displays and branded environments.

Call 087 246 0416

Print made for real business use

Digital printing makes it possible to reproduce colour, photography, detailed graphics and campaign artwork at a scale that works in physical spaces. The important part is matching the material and finish to where the graphic will be used.

Typical applications

  • Printed sign panels
  • Window and wall graphics
  • Promotional posters
  • Event and display graphics
  • Vehicle graphics
  • Short-run branded materials

Artwork prepared at the right scale

Large-format work exposes weak artwork quickly. Resolution, colour, bleed and final dimensions should be checked before production so text remains sharp and images hold up at viewing distance.

Choose the material around the environment

An indoor display and an exterior sign have different requirements. The substrate, adhesive, laminate and finish should be chosen for the surface, expected life and exposure to weather or handling.

Part of a complete signage system

Digital print often works alongside cut vinyl, fabricated lettering and display hardware. Apex can coordinate these pieces so the entire job feels like one solution rather than separate print orders.
