Exhibition & Displays

In a busy event space a business has seconds to be noticed. A coordinated display system makes the stand recognisable from across the room and professional when visitors arrive.

Call 087 246 0416

Build a recognisable event presence

Exhibitions are visually competitive. A strong stand uses clear brand elements, one main message and coordinated display pieces so visitors understand who the company is before they walk over.

Display options

  • Pull-up and roller banners
  • Display walls and backdrops
  • Branded counters and panels
  • Event graphics
  • Portable promotional signage
  • Supporting printed material

Design the pieces as one system

A backdrop, counter, banner and sign should feel like parts of the same environment. Designing them together avoids mismatched scale and makes even a compact stand look more intentional.

Reusable where it makes sense

Core brand display pieces can be designed without event-specific dates so they work repeatedly, while smaller panels and printed graphics can be changed for individual campaigns.

Plan for setup and transport

Portable display equipment needs to work beyond the design file. Size, weight, assembly and storage should be considered when choosing the right format for the team using it.
