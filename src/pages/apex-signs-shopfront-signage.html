Shopfront Signage

The front of the premises is often the first physical impression of the business. Strong shopfront signage makes it easier to find, easier to recognise and more professional before the customer reaches the door.

Call 087 246 0416

Make the premises work for the brand

A shopfront sign has to work with the building, not fight it. Letter size, mounting height, lighting, colour and viewing distance should all be considered together.

Popular shopfront options

  • Built-up and raised lettering
  • Illuminated letters and lightboxes
  • Fascia signs and panels
  • Projecting signs
  • Window graphics and opening information
  • Coordinated door and entrance branding

Readable by day and night

For businesses trading into the evening, illumination can make the difference between a sign that disappears and one that continues to work. Lighting should improve recognition without making the frontage visually noisy.

One visual system

The fascia, projecting sign, windows and entrance should feel like one brand. Coordinating these pieces produces a much more expensive and established impression than treating each item separately.

Designed, produced and fitted

Apex can take a shopfront from the first concept through manufacture and installation, helping the final result stay consistent with the approved design.
