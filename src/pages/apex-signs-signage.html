Signage built around the business

A sign should do more than fill a space on a wall. The scale, materials, contrast and positioning all affect whether the business is noticed and remembered. Apex Signs designs signage around the actual location and the way customers approach it.

Types of signage

  • Exterior business and building signs
  • Raised and built-up lettering
  • Illuminated signage
  • Directional and wayfinding signs
  • Printed panels and promotional signs
  • Health, safety and information signage

Design that reads quickly

People often see a sign while walking or driving past. Clear hierarchy and strong contrast matter more than trying to fit every possible message into the space.

Materials chosen for the job

Indoor reception signage has different demands from an exposed exterior sign. The right substrate, finish, fixing and lighting method should be chosen around where the sign will live.

From concept to installation

Apex can handle the process from artwork and production through to fitting, so the finished sign looks like the design that was approved.
