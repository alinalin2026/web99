Signage

Good signage does more than put a name on a wall. It tells people who you are, what you do and where to go — before anyone has to ask.

Call 087 246 0416

What signage can do for the business

Indoor, outdoor and bespoke signage designed to make the business clear, recognisable and easy to find. The aim is not simply to fill space with graphics. It is to make the business easier to recognise, easier to understand and more professional wherever the customer sees it.

Typical applications

  • External business signs and fascia signage
  • Dimensional lettering and logo signs
  • Illuminated and non-illuminated sign concepts
  • Internal signs, reception branding and wayfinding
  • Design, production and installation planning

Signage that earns its space

A business sign is working every hour the premises is visible. The strongest sign systems are simple at a distance, recognisable at a glance and consistent with the rest of the brand. Apex Signs can take a project from an initial idea or existing logo through the practical stages of sizing, materials, production and installation.

Designed for the location, not just the screen

A sign that looks good on a laptop can fail once it is ten metres away, viewed from traffic or surrounded by competing shopfronts. The design needs the right contrast, letter size, proportions and lighting for the actual environment. That is why the process starts with the location and the customer journey, not with decoration.

A complete route from concept to install

For businesses that do not want to coordinate a designer, printer, fabricator and installer separately, a single signage partner keeps the project simpler. The goal is one visual direction, one specification and one finished result that looks intentional.

Start with the job, not a long email chain
