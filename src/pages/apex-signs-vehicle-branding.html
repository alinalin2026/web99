Vehicle Branding

Your van is already travelling through the areas where you work. Vehicle graphics turn those journeys, site visits and parked hours into repeated brand exposure.

Call 087 246 0416

Turn the vehicle into a mobile billboard

Effective vehicle branding makes the business name, service and contact route clear at a glance. The design should work when the vehicle is parked, moving and viewed from different angles.

Vehicle branding options

  • Vinyl-cut logos and lettering
  • Printed vehicle graphics
  • Partial wraps
  • Full vehicle wraps
  • Reflective graphics for visibility after dark
  • Fleet branding systems for multiple vehicles

Designed for the shape of the vehicle

Doors, handles, windows and body lines affect where important information can go. Artwork should be laid out on the actual vehicle template so the strongest brand elements stay readable after fitting.

Keep the message simple

A vehicle is not a brochure. A strong logo, clear service cue, phone number and web address usually work harder than a wall of text. The goal is recognition in seconds.

A consistent fleet looks bigger

When several vans share the same visual system, a small fleet can create the impression of a much larger presence. Apex can keep colours, logo placement and service messaging consistent across different vehicle sizes.
