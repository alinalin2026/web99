Window Graphics

Glass is valuable visual space. Window graphics can add branding, promote an offer, provide privacy or completely change how a shopfront or office looks from the street.

Call 087 246 0416

Turn empty glass into useful brand space

Window graphics let a business add visual impact without changing the structure of the building. They can be subtle and architectural or bold and promotional depending on what the space needs to do.

Window graphic options

  • Cut vinyl lettering and logos
  • Full-colour printed graphics
  • Frosted privacy film
  • Opening hours and contact details
  • Promotional campaign graphics
  • Large shop-window branding

Balance visibility and privacy

Some businesses want to keep natural light and visibility while adding branding. Others need privacy for offices, treatment rooms or meeting areas. The coverage and material can be adjusted around the practical use of the glass.

Design from the street view

Window graphics should be designed for how customers actually see them — from across a pavement, through reflections and often while moving past the premises.

Easy to coordinate with shopfront signage

Matching window graphics to fascia signs, projecting signs and entrance branding creates a stronger overall frontage and gives campaigns more room to communicate without overcrowding the main sign.
