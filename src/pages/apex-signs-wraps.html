Wraps & Partial Wraps

A well-designed wrap can transform an ordinary vehicle into a piece of brand identity that customers remember.

Call 087 246 0416

What wraps & partial wraps can do for the business

Bold vehicle graphics for businesses that want more visual impact than a simple door logo. The aim is not simply to fill space with graphics. It is to make the business easier to recognise, easier to understand and more professional wherever the customer sees it.

Typical applications

  • Full-wrap visual concepts
  • Partial wraps using vehicle colour as part of the design
  • Large-format branded graphics
  • Commercial van and car applications
  • Layouts focused on visibility rather than clutter

Full impact or strategic coverage

Not every vehicle needs to be covered edge to edge. A partial wrap can use the original paint colour as negative space and still create a strong result. Full wraps allow the design to control almost the entire visual surface. The right option depends on the vehicle, budget and how dramatic the brand needs to be.

Design for motion

A vehicle is rarely viewed like a poster. People see it moving, parked at an angle or passing in traffic. The strongest wrap layouts use large shapes, short messages and recognisable brand elements that still work in those conditions.

Professional finish matters

The difference between a great concept and a great finished wrap is execution. Panel preparation, alignment around doors and curves, clean edges and careful finishing all affect how professional the result feels up close.
