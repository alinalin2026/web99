Wraps & Partial Wraps

When simple lettering is not enough, a partial or full wrap gives the vehicle far more visual impact while keeping the design controlled and professional.

Call 087 246 0416

More coverage, more impact

Vehicle wraps give designers more freedom to use colour, photography, shape and large brand elements across the bodywork. A partial wrap can achieve much of the same impact while leaving more of the original paint visible.

What Apex can wrap

  • Commercial vans
  • Cars and service vehicles
  • Fleet vehicles
  • Partial panels and colour sections
  • Full printed wraps
  • Promotional and campaign graphics

Design first, fitting second

A wrap should be planned around panel joins, handles, lights, curves and viewing angles before anything is printed. That preparation prevents key parts of the design from disappearing into awkward areas of the vehicle.

Professional application matters

Preparation and fitting determine how the finished wrap looks. Clean edges, consistent tension and careful work around recesses create the difference between a temporary-looking graphic and a professional result.

Use the wrap to simplify the message

More printable area does not mean more text is needed. Strong wraps use scale and colour to create recognition first, then make contact details easy to find.
