# WestPrint3D — Website Design Direction

## Three Visual Approaches Considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Forge | A low-light industrial atelier where electric lime reveals precision-made objects from the darkness. Tactile, technical, and intentionally restrained. | 0.07 |
| Workshop Field Notes | A documentary-inspired maker journal with bright paper, annotated imagery, and quiet craft confidence. More editorial and approachable than technical. | 0.03 |
| Archive of Objects | A museum-like black gallery, presenting each printed object as a designed artifact through generous space and measured typography. | 0.09 |

## Chosen Approach: Signal Forge

### Design Movement

**Contemporary industrial minimalism** informed by precision toolmaking, black anodized surfaces, and exhibition-grade product photography.

### Core Principles

1. **Product first:** the supplied objects are treated as engineered artifacts, not generic catalogue thumbnails.
2. **Precision over spectacle:** lime is a calibration signal and a directional guide, never a full-page glow effect.
3. **Measured asymmetry:** editorial offsets, staggered image crops, and non-uniform section rhythms create motion without visual noise.
4. **Tactile depth:** subtle scanlines, fine borders, soft vignettes, and layered blacks evoke the material character of FDM printing.

### Color Philosophy

The site is built on a near-black graphite field that lets the supplied black product photography blend into the canvas. **West Signal Green** provides a controlled, high-contrast highlight for pathways, selected controls, product identifiers, and fine edges. A warm off-white softens long-form reading while mineral grey handles secondary information.

### Layout Paradigm

The homepage follows a **production-line narrative** rather than a centered marketing stack. A wide, left-aligned opening message is balanced by an offset hero image; subsequent sections shift between full-bleed gallery moments and narrow editorial copy rails. A vertical specification rail ties key sections together on desktop.

### Signature Elements

1. **The Westline:** a thin electric-lime rule that travels through sections and becomes a product-card reveal.
2. **Layer marks:** quiet horizontal lines that reference a printer’s stacked layers and appear in labels, cards, and the footer.
3. **Calibration frames:** squared off, inset outlines around selected imagery and calls to action.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should feel like adjusting a well-made tool: clear, firm, and restrained. Links reveal a lime under-rule; product imagery gently lifts on hover; buttons depress subtly rather than floating or bouncing.

### Animation

Sections enter through an 8px upward fade with short, staggered delays. Image hover states use only transform and opacity: a slow 2% scale plus an overlay line sweep. The hero’s light beam is static under reduced-motion preferences and moves only subtly for other visitors. All standard interaction motion stays under 240ms.

### Typography System

**Barlow Condensed** is the compact, engineered display face used in high-contrast all-caps labels and oversized product headlines. **Manrope** provides the readable body system, using 400 and 500 for text, 600 for actions, and 700 for small specifications. Headlines use tight tracking; utility labels use wider letter spacing.

### Brand Essence

**WestPrint3D makes useful, personal objects for organized homes, workshops, and play spaces—built to fit the way people actually live.**

**Personality:** precise, capable, considered.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and material-aware; calls to action invite a concrete next step rather than relying on generic marketing language.

> “Make space work harder.”

> “Bring the object in your head to your hands.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **layered W monogram**, assembled from three descending print layers and contained in an open calibration frame. It reads as a W from a distance and a dimensional contour when inspected closely. The companion wordmark uses a condensed, engineered letterform with the “3D” picked out in West Signal Green.

### Signature Brand Color

**West Signal Green — #B6FF32**

## Style Decisions

- Use the supplied product imagery as the primary proof of craft and keep it large enough to show material texture.
- Avoid rounded, app-like cards; use light borders and occasional angled offsets instead.
- Maintain high contrast: white or warm white text is only placed over dark imagery with an explicit charcoal overlay.
- No fabricated reviews, ratings, customer names, or testimonials are included.
- West Signal Green #B6FF32 is a calibration signal only: use it for rules, identifiers, selected headline words, primary actions, and product-edge highlights, never as a large background field.
- The Westline operates as one continuous visual system across the homepage through thin lime rules, layer marks, calibration frames, and product-card reveals.
- Dark graphite remains the dominant atmosphere; warm off-white sections are brief editorial/specification pauses and retain industrial rails or layer marks.
