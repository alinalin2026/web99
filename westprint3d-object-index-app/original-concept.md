## Chosen Direction: The Object Index

### Design Movement

**Memphis-inflected editorial modernism** with the order and quiet confidence of a well-designed design annual. The result is collectible and playful without becoming childish.

### Core Principles

1. **A useful object can also be visually joyful:** spotlight practical 3D printed pieces as small design statements.
2. **Bright fields, precise structure:** generous cream space is anchored by strict rules, index numbers, and modular image windows.
3. **Unexpected balance:** oversized serif messages meet compact monospaced specification text.
4. **Material with personality:** visible print layers and translucent plastics are celebrated rather than hidden.

### Color Philosophy

Warm paper (#F4F0E7) is the dominant canvas, giving the work a gallery-catalogue feel. **Index Blue** (#2453FF) is a confident navigational signal; persimmon (#F15B3B), butter (#F0C94C), and soft lilac (#DCCAF7) arrive in generous color blocks to distinguish categories and make the experience feel like a collection rather than a shop template.

### Layout Paradigm

This homepage is a **catalogue in motion**: an offset title card meets a sprawling product still-life; long horizontal collection bands interrupt vertical editorial pages; every section is numbered and separated by a page-rule. The layout deliberately changes rhythm rather than stacking near-identical modules.

### Signature Elements

1. **Index dots:** stacked round color chips identify a collection, material, or idea.
2. **Cut-corner frames:** image and text panels have one folded or clipped corner, echoing a physical page tab.
3. **The object number:** a large outlined index number appears behind key content moments.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions have the tactility of sorting cards on a studio table. Product panels shift a few pixels, index dots rotate, and links reveal a dotted underline. Motion remains short, legible, and non-gimmicky.

### Animation

Headlines and images enter on a staggered vertical rise. Color panels reveal through clipped masks, and index dots use a tiny 8-degree rotation on hover. Motion stays under 300ms, uses custom easing, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** is used only for memorable editorial statements and italic emphasis. **Space Grotesk** handles every functional headline and interface item, while **IBM Plex Mono** provides dimensions, collection codes, and small descriptors. This mix makes the work feel both curatorial and engineered.

### Brand Essence

**WestPrint3D turns the overlooked spaces in your day into an editable collection of useful objects.**

**Personality:** bright, clever, precise.

### Brand Voice

The tone is concise, observant, and slightly unexpected. Headlines act like useful labels rather than big claims.

> “The small things, sorted.”

> “A better place for the bits of your day.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **three-layer cube-tab**: a compact isometric cube whose front face carries a single W-shaped print path. It is paired with a restrained sans-serif wordmark where the “3D” is treated as a small superscript catalogue code rather than a loud decoration.

### Signature Brand Color

**Index Blue — #2453FF**

## Style Decisions

- No dark graphite or electric-lime heritage from the prior concept carries forward.
- The supplied imagery is not reused in the new composition; fresh art direction is the point of this version.
- Cream is the dominant background and color appears in clear collection blocks, not decorative gradients.
- No fabricated reviews, ratings, or customer quotations are used.
